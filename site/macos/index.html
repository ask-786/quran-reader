Install guide

Quran Reader for Mac

A free Quran app for macOS that works without internet, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs alike. Read the Madani Mushaf page by page or a whole Surah at once, with recitation, Tafsīr Ibn Kathīr and al-Jalālayn, and daily reading routines.

Download for Mac (.dmg)

Install

  1. Download the disk image (.dmg) above and open it.
  2. Drag Quran Reader into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Quran Reader from Applications. macOS will say it can't verify the developer. Close that message. This happens because the app isn't code-signed yet.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section and click Open Anyway next to the Quran Reader message. Confirm with your password.

You only need to do this once. After that, Quran Reader opens like any other app.

If you're comfortable with Terminal, this does the same as steps 3 and 4:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Quran Reader.app"

Update

Download the newest disk image and drag Quran Reader into Applications again, replacing the old copy. Your reading position, history, bookmarks and routines are kept.

Uninstall

Drag Quran Reader from Applications to the Bin.

What you get

The full Quran text and all 604 Mushaf pages built in, so it reads with no connection. Focus mode goes full screen to leave only the text, alongside auto-scroll at your own pace, your reading history, recitation from five reciters, Tafsīr Ibn Kathīr and al-Jalālayn in Arabic and English, and daily routines.
