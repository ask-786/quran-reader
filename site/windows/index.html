Quran Reader for Windows

A free Quran app for Windows 10 and 11 that works without internet. Read the Madani Mushaf page by page or a whole Surah at once, with recitation, Tafsīr Ibn Kathīr and al-Jalālayn, and daily reading routines.

Download for Windows (.exe) MSI package

Install

  1. Download the installer (.exe) above.
  2. Open it. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. The warning appears because the installer isn't code-signed yet, not because something is wrong with it.
  3. Follow the installer. Quran Reader is then in your Start menu.

Quran Reader uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 11 already has. On an older Windows 10 computer that is missing it, the installer downloads it once.

The .msi package installs the same app and suits installing on several computers at once, for example through Group Policy.

Update

Download the newest installer from this page and run it. Your reading position, history, bookmarks and routines are kept.

Uninstall

Open Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Quran Reader and choose Uninstall.

What you get

The full Quran text and all 604 Mushaf pages built in, so it reads with no connection. Focus mode and full screen (F11) for reading without distraction, auto-scroll at your own pace, your reading history, recitation from five reciters, Tafsīr Ibn Kathīr and al-Jalālayn in Arabic and English, and daily routines.
