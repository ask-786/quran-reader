Offline first

Your Quran doesn't need the internet

Most Quran apps load the text from a server, which means they stop working when your connection does. Quran Reader carries the complete text and all 604 Mushaf pages inside the app, so it opens and reads the same on a plane, in a masjid with no signal or on a computer that never goes online.

  • Works from the first launch. No sign-up, no setup, no “downloading data” screen.
  • Downloads only when you ask. Recitation and tafsir are downloaded the first time you ask for them, then kept for offline use.
  • You decide what's kept. Settings shows how much space audio takes, per reciter, and clears it in one click.
  • Nothing leaves your computer. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your reading history stays on your computer.

Made for reading

Everything here exists to keep you with the text, and out of menus.

Distraction-free reading

Press f for focus mode: the toolbar and sidebar disappear and only the verses remain. Go full screen with F11 (on a Mac, focus mode does it for you). Pick a dark, light or sepia theme that suits the room.

Auto-scroll

Press a and the page moves on by itself, so your hands can rest. Drag the speed handle, or use Shift+↑ / ↓, until it matches your pace. Space pauses.

Reading history

The app opens where you stopped. Every Surah, Juz, Hizb and page remembers your place on its own, and the Recent list keeps each sitting with the verses it covered, so you can go back to any of them.

Recitation

Five reciters, all Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim. Hear a single verse when a word doesn't sit right, repeat it a set number of times with a pause to say it back, or listen to a whole Surah. Save a Surah's audio to play offline.

Tafsir

Tafsīr Ibn Kathīr and Tafsīr al-Jalālayn, each in Arabic and in English. Download an edition once and it sits beside the verse you are reading, offline, following you as you go. Each is labelled with its school of fiqh and its creed, so you know how it reads the verses.

Daily routines

Make a routine like “Morning: Yā-Sīn, Al-Wāqiʿah, Al-Mulk” and read it as one set. The end of each Surah leads to the next in your routine, the toolbar shows how far along you are today, and every routine starts fresh the next day.

Also in the app

  • Mushaf page view: all 604 Madani Mushaf pages with the printed line breaks, in the official KFGQPC fonts
  • Scrolling reader: a whole Surah in one view, with page and juz markers
  • Jump anywhere: Ctrl+K, then type 255, 2:255 or p255
  • Browse by Surah, Juz, Hizb or page
  • Bookmark and copy any verse
  • Duʿāʾ Khatm al-Qurʾān after An-Nās

Questions

Is Quran Reader free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license, with no ads, no account and no paid tier. The source code is on GitHub.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. The Quran text and the Mushaf fonts come with the app, so reading never needs a connection. Recitation and tafsir are downloaded once, the first time you ask for them, and work offline after that.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when I open it?

The builds aren't code-signed yet. Signing certificates cost money every year, and until the project has them your system can't confirm who made the installer. The Windows and macOS install guides show how to open it anyway.

Which Mushaf and text does it use?

The page view follows the Madani Mushaf (604 pages) in the King Fahd Complex (KFGQPC) QCF v4 fonts. The scrolling reader uses the Uthmani text from the Tanzil Project.

Is there a phone or tablet version?

Not at the moment. Quran Reader runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Can I search the Quran or read a translation?

Not yet. Search and translations are planned. You can follow progress or suggest features on GitHub.
